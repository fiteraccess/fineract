## Rule:
Work with me like a pair programmer. Implement one module at a time. Before writing any code, summarize your
understanding, propose a short plan, and ask for confirmation. After each module, wait for my approval before moving to
the next.

If requirements are unclear, ask 1–3 targeted questions.
Keep each response short, actionable, and end with a clear decision point.
